# IMAGE STRATEGY

## OVERVIEW
The visual direction requires high-end, editorial healthcare photography that feels premium, architectural, and authentically Canadian. The images must evoke trust, calm, and precision without feeling like generic stock photos.

## GUIDING PRINCIPLES
- **Style**: 35mm / 50mm editorial perspective, natural window light, subtle depth of field.
- **Tones**: Real skin tones, controlled soft highlights, avoiding sterile blue "hospital" atmospheres.
- **Subjects**: Calm human interaction, no exaggerated smiles. Diverse Calgary community representation without stereotyping.
- **Reality Constraints**: 
  - Never fabricate a fake physician and present them as a real Rundle doctor.
  - When enhancing the real clinic, preserve actual architecture, room dimensions, and equipment. Do not invent a fictional luxury space.

## IMAGE CATEGORIES
1. **REAL_CLINIC_ENHANCEMENT**: Using existing clinic photos (from the WordPress site) and putting them through the NEXORA pipeline to improve lighting, color balance, composition, and polish while retaining factual reality.
2. **EDITORIAL_HEALTHCARE**: Stylized, premium representations of healthcare interactions (used only when clear it's not specific staff identity).
3. **REAL_STAFF_PHOTO_REQUIRED**: Slots reserved specifically for actual, approved physician and team headshots. Placeholder identity-neutral cards will be used until real portraits are supplied.
4. **ABSTRACT_GRAPHIC**: Custom SVGs or subtle generated textures (continuous care lines, ECG-inspired geometry) rather than photography.
