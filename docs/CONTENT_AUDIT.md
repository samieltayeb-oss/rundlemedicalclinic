# CONTENT AUDIT

## HOMEPAGE (`/`)
- **Strengths**: Mentions core services, has testimonials, introduces the team.
- **Weaknesses**: Uses generic claims ("Quality Treatment", "Best Service", "Best Children Care"). Some conflicting information regarding Sunday hours. Unorganized layout with repetitive text.

## ABOUT US (`/about-us/`)
- **Key Facts**: Emphasizes male and female family physicians, on-site female pediatrician, and on-site pharmacy with free delivery. PCN (Primary Care Network) integration is mentioned.
- **Action**: Needs editorial rewrite to improve flow and sound more premium, eliminating repetitive phrasing.

## SERVICES (`/services/`)
- **Structure**: It's a massive, unorganized list spanning Primary Care, Specialty Care, Diagnostics, Chronic Disease, Minor Procedures, Mental Health, Rehab, Men's/Women's health, WCB, Travel Health, etc.
- **Action**: This must be completely re-architected into category-specific pages (e.g., `/services/family-medicine`, `/services/pediatrics`, `/services/minor-procedures`) with progressive disclosure. Remove the "laundry list" format.

## DOCTORS/TEAM (`/doctors/`)
- **Current State**: Basic cards showing Name, Role, Gender. Mentions PCN integration (Health Management Nurse/Pharmacist, Behavioral Health Consultant). Includes Front Desk Staff and an IT Manager.
- **Action**: Redesign as an elegant physician showcase. PCN members and support staff should be categorized separately. Build individual detail routes (`/team/[slug]`) for future-proofing.

## FAQ (`/faq/`)
- **Content**: Covers annual physicals, walk-in vs. appointments, wait times, specialists, PCN affiliation, and uninsured services.
- **Action**: Needs to be integrated into an elegant accordion system. Uninsured services list is good but should be placed in the "New Patients" section.

## CONTACT (`/contact-us/`)
- **Current State**: Lacks structured information in the main body (mostly found in the footer/header). Mentions a temporary closure in the footer.
- **Action**: Needs a dramatic improvement. Add interactive map, clear hours table, tap-to-call, directions, and a Smart Notice System for closures instead of footer text.

## OVERALL COPY TONE
- **Current**: Generic, slightly repetitive, and utilizes typical medical marketing hype ("Best Service", "Qualified Doctors"). Grammar needs refinement.
- **Target**: Warm, clear, credible, reassuring, human, concise, professional.
